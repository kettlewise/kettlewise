Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Everything you put into Kettlewise stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we never see your data.

This policy covers the Kettlewise kettlebell training app for Android and Wear OS ("Kettlewise", "we"). It explains what the app does with information about you.

What the app stores, and where

You can enter a profile: a name if you choose, age, sex, height, weight, training experience, goals, schedule, physical limitations and answers to a health screening questionnaire. The app also records the kettlebells you own, your workouts and effort ratings, body measurements, progress photos you add and video clips you film.

All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Progress photos and filmed clips are also encrypted with a key held in your phone's secure hardware. Kettlewise has no server and needs no account. We cannot see, access or recover any of your data.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to film a set. Clips stay on your device, encrypted, unless you export or share one yourself.

Motion sensors and watch

If you turn on rep counting, the app reads the motion sensors of your phone or Wear OS watch during a set to count reps and estimate bell speed. The phone and watch communicate directly through Google's Wear OS connection. These readings are processed on the device as you train and are not stored or sent anywhere else.

Health Connect (optional)

Only if you turn it on, Kettlewise reads your heart rate during workouts. It also writes finished workouts and estimated active calories to Health Connect. You can withdraw access at any time in Health Connect settings. Data from Health Connect is used only to show it to you during your workout and is never shared with anyone. Kettlewise's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Purchases

Kettlewise Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. The app only receives confirmation that you own Pro, and we never receive your payment details. Google Play's billing component, which is built into the app, may send Google diagnostic information about purchases. If you receive a gift code, it is checked on your device and is not sent anywhere.

Backups

You can save a backup file of your data. It is encrypted with a password you choose and saved wherever you decide. We never receive it, and without your password no one can open it, including us.

No advertising, analytics or tracking

Kettlewise contains no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party data collection. We do not sell or share your data.

Children

Kettlewise is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Settings, then Delete everything, erases all your data from the app immediately. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Health information

Kettlewise gives general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise programme, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or chest discomfort.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
